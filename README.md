# Standard-Chartered-Credit-Analyst-Job-Simulation
Credit Analyst Job Simulation by Standard Chartered through Forage!

## Intro & Scenario
Background context and your project team

Your role
- You are working as a Credit Analyst at Standard Chartered.
- You are part of a team responsible for carrying out the annual credit review of Green Solutions Manufacturing Ltd, one of our long-standing clients.
- The client is a market-leading manufacturer of sustainable drinkware (including reusable water bottles and coffee cups). It is looking to borrow money to fund its sustainability initiatives.
- This role will allow you to carry out quantitative and qualitative credit assessments of a client in a practical setting.


Your goal
- Assist your project team in completing the annual credit review of Green Solutions Manufacturing Ltd. 
- You will need to conduct a financial health analysis and industry research and present your findings to your manager and credit officer.

---

## Task 1: Financial health analysis
Unveiling credit strength behind the numbers
- Assess the provided financial statements of Green Solutions Manufacturing Ltd to determine their current financial health.
- Analyse key financial ratios to assess potential risks faced by the client.
- Produce a preliminary report highlighting the client’s financial position and potential risks.

---

## Task 2: Industry research
Piecing together market trends to assess the overall creditworthiness of a client
- Perform industry research in order to understand macroeconomic trends that may impact Green Solutions Manufacturing Ltd. 
- Compile your analysis into a video presentation, summarising your findings from current and previous tasks, and their implications on Green Solutions Manufacturing Ltd’s creditworthiness.

---

## Conclusion
During this programme, you’ll have the opportunity to step into the shoes of a Standard Chartered Credit Analyst and complete tasks that replicate the work that they do every day. You’ll learn key skills in risk identification, quantitative analysis, credit analysis, critical thinking and presenting. 

This programme has been designed to help you upskill and strengthen your resume as you explore your career options, and a potential career at Standard Chartered.
